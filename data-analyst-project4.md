## Project Description
A sales analysis project focused on understanding coffee shop performance, transaction activity, product sales, and revenue trends using a retail sales dataset.

## Tools Used
Microsoft Excel, PivotTables, formulas, data cleaning, data visualization

## My Approach
Cleaned and organized the raw sales data, standardized information, used formulas and PivotTables to summarize the data, and analyzed sales performance across products, stores, transactions, and price ranges.

## Goals
Converted raw coffee shop sales data into a structured analysis that highlighted key sales trends, product performance, transaction activity, and store-level performance.
