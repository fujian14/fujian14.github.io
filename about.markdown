My journey into data analytics started with a background in hospitality and tourism, where I developed strong communication, organization, problem-solving, and customer-focused skills. While working and learning in different environments, I became increasingly interested in how data can be used to understand patterns, identify problems, and support better decisions. That interest led me to begin transitioning my career into data analytics and build my skills through self-directed learning and hands-on projects. 

As I began exploring the field, I focused on learning the tools and practical skills used by Data Analysts. Rather than only studying theory, I challenged myself to work with real datasets and build projects from start to finish. Through these projects, I have practiced cleaning and preparing messy data, exploring trends and patterns, writing SQL queries, creating visualizations, and turning analytical findings into clear and understandable insights.

My previous experience has also given me transferable skills that I bring into analytics, including communication, attention to detail, teamwork, organization, and the ability to understand problems from different perspectives. I’m now combining those skills with my growing technical knowledge to build a career in data analytics.

## Tools & Skills
I use SQL, Excel, Tableau, Power BI, and Python to analyze and visualize data. My projects have given me hands-on experience with data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, data visualization, dashboard development, filtering and aggregating data, identifying trends, and communicating findings.

My portfolio projects are designed around practical business questions rather than simply demonstrating technical skills. I have worked with datasets involving areas such as freelancer data and coffee shop sales, where I cleaned raw data, explored patterns and trends, analyzed performance, and created dashboards to make the results easier to understand.

## My goal
I’m still at the beginning of my professional journey in data analytics, therefore I’m committed to continuously learning and improving. I’m looking for an opportunity where I can contribute my analytical, communication, and problem-solving skills while gaining real-world experience and learning from an experienced team.

My goal is simple: turn data into insights that help people understand problems, make informed decisions, create better outcomes, and gain professional experiences.
